Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Kolidiscope platform, onboarding, data handling, and security.

Platform & Product
Kolidiscope is a SaaS platform built for data vendors in financial services. We automate prospective data studies, optimize data product waterfalls, monitor scoring model performance, track attribute-level metrics, and generate customer-ready reports — all designed to help you sell and support your data products more efficiently.
We serve data vendors and alternative data providers in the financial services industry — specifically those selling data products used in credit decisioning, risk assessment, fraud detection, and similar workflows.
The platform runs prospective performance studies that evaluate your data products against customer portfolios. This includes measuring predictive lift, population coverage, KS statistics, segment-level analysis, and ROI quantification. Studies can be run on demand and results are delivered as customer-ready reports.
Kolidiscope analyzes your data product configurations and optimizes waterfall ordering and decision cutoffs based on customer-specific constraints. Customers can optimize for higher approval volume, tighter risk tolerance, or reduced data cost — and the platform recommends the best configuration for their use case.
We continuously monitor scoring model performance and individual attribute metrics over time. The platform tracks model drift, validation statistics, and attribute-level changes so you can identify issues early and maintain confidence with your customers.

Security & Compliance

About

Kolidiscope, Inc.

Kolidiscope, Inc. is a technology company focused on the financial data industry. We build products that solve a problem every data vendor knows: proving and optimizing the value of data products takes too long and costs too much.

Our flagship product, Pareskope, automates the analytical heavy lifting — waterfall optimization, prospective studies, model monitoring, and reporting — so data vendors can sell more effectively, onboard customers faster, and demonstrate ongoing value with confidence.
